Landscape · Ecology · Research

Field Notes Studio is an emerging landscape design practice exploring the relationships between land, water, and everyday life. We approach each project as an opportunity to look closely: to understand the systems, memories, and patterns of use that give a place its character. Our work moves between landscape architecture, ecological research, and spatial experimentation, connecting the scale of a wider territory with the experience of a single path, planting, or threshold.

We believe landscapes are never finished objects. They grow, weather, and change through the people and living systems that shape them. Our design philosophy begins with what is already there, asking how careful interventions can strengthen a site’s ecological and social possibilities. We are interested in resilient public spaces, native planting communities, material reuse, and the quiet qualities that invite people to pause, gather, and return.

Our methodology combines field observation, drawing, mapping, model making, and conversation. Research and design develop together: an early question may become a planting study, a material test, or a new way of organising space. We are currently developing projects and research around water, community landscapes, and the gradual transformation of urban ground. Working with collaborators across disciplines, we aim to make places that remain open to adaptation, care, and discovery.
