“Regain the Voice” addresses the historical muting of minority voices in Chinatown and its adjacencies, areas impacted by the imposition of megastructures like highways.

This project reinterprets the concept of “voice” to encompass both the literal soundscapes and the figurative influence of the community. Applying three strategic approaches—buffer, connect, and amplify—the initiative transforms the traffic-heavy muted ground into pedestrian-friendly zones with ecological buffers and cultural activities. It enhances urban connectivity, fosters community interaction, and restores the area’s role as a vibrant public agora, offering a refuge from urban noise and a platform for communal expression.
